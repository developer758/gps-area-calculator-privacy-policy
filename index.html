Privacy Policy

GPS Area Calculator

By Integrated Geo-Informatics Solutions Pvt Ltd

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Integrated Geo-Informatics Solutions Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") provides the GPS Area Calculator Android application (the "App"). The App is designed to help surveyors, farmers, civil engineers, real estate agents, landowners, and other users mark land boundaries and calculate plot areas and perimeters using GPS coordinates.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the App, how it is used and stored, which third-party services are integrated, and how you can manage or delete your data.

Privacy at a glance

Your field measurements and saved plot information are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a developer server that receives or stores your GPS measurements. Location access is used while the App is actively in use, not for background tracking. Google Maps and Google Play Services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App processes location coordinates and field measurement information to provide its core functionality. We do not collect personal information for storage on our own remote servers through the App.

1.1 Location Data and GPS Permissions

The App requests foreground location permissions on supported Android devices:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — allows the App to access precise location information when granted.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — allows the App to access approximate location information when granted.

Location information is used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your current position on Google Maps.
  • Marking GPS points along a land or plot boundary.
  • Calculating polygon area and perimeter in real time using recorded boundary coordinates.
  • Saving coordinates associated with field measurements so you can view your plots later.

Foreground use only: Location access is intended for use while you actively use the App. The App does not intentionally track your location in the background.

Local processing: GPS coordinates are processed on your device for boundary marking and calculations. Saved measurement coordinates remain in the App's local database and are not uploaded to our developer servers.

Location access can be denied or changed at any time through Android Settings. Some GPS-based features will not work correctly without the required permission.

1.2 Local Data Storage

The App uses a local SQLite database managed through Android Room to store information required for field measurement features.

Depending on how you use the App, locally stored information may include:

  • Plot or field names entered by the user.
  • Latitude and longitude coordinates of boundary markers.
  • Calculated polygon areas and perimeters.
  • Other measurement details needed to display saved plots.

This information is retained locally on your device. It is not automatically transmitted to our remote servers or sold to third parties.

Where database encryption is implemented and enabled, locally stored records are protected using that encryption. Please note that the level of protection also depends on your device security, Android configuration, and whether you export an unencrypted backup.

1.3 Storage Permissions and Database Backups

On Android versions where applicable, the App may use storage permissions such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE to support its database backup feature.

  • Purpose: Exporting a backup of your saved measurement database as a ZIP file.
  • Destination: The Downloads folder on your device, subject to the Android version and available file access mechanisms.
  • User control: Backup creation is initiated by the user.
  • These permissions are not intended to collect your personal files for analytics or advertising.

The exact storage permissions available and required depend on your Android version. On newer Android versions, the App may use scoped storage or the system file picker instead of legacy storage permissions.

Exported backup files are outside the App's private storage. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read its contents unless the backup itself is encrypted. Keep backup files in a secure location.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google Play Services and the Google Maps SDK to provide map rendering and location-related functionality.

These services are operated by Google and may process technical information, device information, network information, location-related information, or service diagnostics as necessary to provide their features. Such processing is governed by Google's own policies and service settings.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform Terms: Google Maps Terms

We do not control the independent data practices of these third-party services. Please review their policies for information about their data handling practices.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your saved field measurements with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. The App does not automatically upload your locally stored plot names, boundary coordinates, calculated areas, or perimeters to our developer servers.

However, third-party services such as Google Maps and Google Play Services may independently process information when you use their functionality. Network communications necessary to load maps or use these services are subject to the applicable third-party privacy policies.

5. Data Security and Retention

Your saved measurements remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the relevant local files.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and installing Android security updates. Device loss, unauthorized device access, or insecure exported backups may expose locally stored information.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files and maintaining copies of important measurements.

6. Data Deletion and User Control

You can control and remove your saved measurement information in the following ways:

  • Delete individual measurements: Use the relevant saved-data or measurement management feature inside the App to delete individual plots, where available.
  • Clear App data: Open Android Settings, navigate to Apps → GPS Area Calculator → Storage, and select Clear storage or Clear data. The exact wording varies by Android version.
  • Remove exported backups: If you created ZIP backups, delete them separately from your Downloads folder or other storage locations.

Clearing App data or uninstalling the App may permanently remove saved measurements that have not been backed up. Clearing the App's cache alone may not delete its database.

7. Children's Privacy

GPS Area Calculator is designed for land measurement, surveying, and related professional or personal uses. It is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for storage on our developer servers. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so that we can review the matter.
